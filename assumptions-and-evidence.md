# Assumptions and Evidence Register

The original CareBridge concept document includes market statistics, revenue estimates, competitor comparisons, and unit-economics projections. Those figures have not been independently verified in this repository.

| Claim / assumption from original concept | How to treat it in the portfolio | Validation required |
|---|---|---|
| 150M+ people aged 60+ in India | Unverified market context | Current authoritative demographic source and year |
| 340M+ people aged 60+ by 2050 | Projection requiring attribution | Original projection source, methodology, and scenario |
| 50–60% chronic-patient medication adherence | Unverified baseline | Study population, geography, condition, and adherence definition |
| Digital healthcare market exceeds $37B by 2030 | Market forecast | Named research source, currency, scope, and publication date |
| TAM ₹64,800 Cr; SAM ₹14,000 Cr; SOM ₹720 Cr | Original estimates, not established market sizing | Bottom-up calculation, target segment, price, reachable users |
| 50,000 paying families equals ₹720 Cr annual revenue | Arithmetic / pricing assumption to reconcile | Price per family, monthly vs annual basis, conversion and retention assumptions |
| ₹999–₹1,999 monthly subscription | Pricing hypothesis | Willingness-to-pay interviews and competitor pricing |
| CAC ₹500–₹1,000; LTV ₹15,000–₹25,000+ | Forecast only | Acquisition channel costs, gross margin, churn, cohort retention |
| Competitor gaps for Practo, Tata 1mg, Emoha, Portea | Hypotheses, not definitive competitive facts | Current feature review, user interviews, dated source links |
| 85% adherence, 95% missed-dose detection, <2-minute alerts | Pilot targets only | Metric definitions, instrumentation, baseline and observed pilot data |

## Portfolio presentation rule
Use wording such as “proposed target,” “market hypothesis,” and “to validate.” Do not describe projected figures as actual traction, validated outcomes, or achieved performance.
